11 · Operating Principles

How I work.

01

Defensibility over speed.

Every rate, every recommendation, every commercial position should trace back to a source. If I can't show the math, I haven't done the work yet.

02

Methodology, then motion.

Build the engine first — the rate book, the playbook, the framework. Then ship at speed. Without methodology, fast becomes expensive.

03

Half technical, half commercial.

Writing SQL, designing pricing logic, and closing six-figure deals are the same job. The product is whatever the client buys, end-to-end.
